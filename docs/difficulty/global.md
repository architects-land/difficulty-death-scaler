# Global difficulty

The global difficulty is split into steps.
The activated steps is determined by the players' deaths.
This difficulty is *for every player*.

Each time a player dies, the number of deaths is increased.
If a step is reached, it is activated.

If no one dies during 12 hours, the latest activated step is disabled.
For example, if you are at 15 deaths and the two latest steps were activated at 10 deaths and 13 
deaths, after the decrease, the number of deaths will be 10.

## Easy steps

**0 death**
Phantoms are disabled. 
All blocks drop after a block explosion or a mob explosion.
Difficulty is easy.
At least 30% of connected players must sleep to skip the night.

**1 death**
Difficulty is normal.

**2 deaths**
At least 70% of connected players must sleep to skip the night.

**3 deaths**
Difficulty is hard.

**5 deaths**
All blocks do not drop after a block explosion like bed (except TNT) or mob explosion like creeper.
Phantoms are back.
(This the vanilla "Hard" difficulty.)

## Ominous steps

**7 deaths**
Increase mobs' follow range by 25%.
Skeletons are more intelligent.

**8 deaths**
Increase fall damage by 25%.

**10 deaths**
Remove one heart.

**12 deaths**
Increase mobs' step height to `1.1`.

**13 deaths**
All blocks do not drop after a TNT explosion.

**15 deaths**
Increase mobs' reinforcement by 25%.
Zombies are more intelligent.

**16 deaths**
Debug screen (F3) is reduced.
You cannot get your coordinates.

**18 deaths**
Increase fall damage by 50%.

## Dangerous steps

**20 deaths**
Remove another heart (2 removed).

**22 deaths**
All players must sleep to skip the night.
Charged creepers start spawning.

**23 deaths**
Increase mobs' follow range by 35%.

**24 deaths**
Disable infinite water source.

**25 deaths**
Increase mobs' reinforcement by 50%.

**26 deaths**
Increase mpbs' step height to `1.5`.

## Evil steps

**28 deaths**
Limit crafting to unlocked recipes.
Piglin brutes start spawning.

**30 deaths**
Enable universal anger.

**32 deaths**
Increase fall damage by 100%.

**33 deaths**
Increase mobs' reinforcement by 75%.

**35 deaths**
Increase mobs' follow range by 100%.
Creepers are buffed.

## No returns steps

**37 deaths**
Never forgive dead players.

**40 deaths**
Disable natural regeneration.
