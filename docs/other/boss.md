# Boss

In vanilla, there are three bosses: the Ender Dragon, the Wither and the Elder Guadrian.
For Difficulty Death Scaler, the Warden is also a boss.

Every boss can be buffed with a netherite ingot (right click on them).
The buff depends of the boss.

When you kill a buffed boss, the [global difficulty](/difficulty/global) is decreased, like no one
dies during 12 hours.

## Buff

The Ender Dragon's stats are not modified by the buff, but its life is increased if there are many
players in the end with a high [global difficulty](/difficulty/global).

The Wither's stats are not modified, but you can't trap it in bedrock: you have to do the real 
fight.

The Elder Guardian:
- has a better knockback resistance
- is smaller
- has more health
- is faster
- do more damage

The Warden:
- is faster
- is angry when it is buffed
