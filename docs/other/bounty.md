# Bounty

If a player has 3% (or less) of the total number of death, a bounty is put on this player.

If a player kill (the killer) the player with the bounty (the killed), they will swap
their [player difficulty](/difficulty/player).
In addition to this, the killer is rewarded with a permanent bonus heart.
Player can have a maximum of 5 bonus hearts.
The killed loses all their bonus hearts.

You can get the list of bounties with the command `/bounty`.

::: tip
The bounty starts between 5 and 10 minutes *after* the connection of the player.

If the server has less than 20 deaths, the bounty will never be introduced.
:::

Player A died once and the server has 50 deaths.
A bounty will be put on Player A between 5 and 10 minutes after his connection.
Player B kills Player A.
Player B receives the player difficulty of Player A and Player A receives the player difficulty of Player B.
